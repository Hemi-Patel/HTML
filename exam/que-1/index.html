Radha Krishna Mashup Songs

Radha and Krishna are Hindu deities and a symbol of divine love. They are considered to be the supreme entities in some sects of Hinduism. Their love is celebrated in many ways, including through festivals, art, and literature.

As per scriptures, Radha is considered as the complete incarnation of Mahalakshmi. It is believed that Krishna enchants the world, but Radha enchants even him. Therefore, she is the supreme goddess of all, and together they are called Radha-Krishna.
